AutoPage — Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

AutoPage turns the pages of your sheet music when you tilt your head. It runs entirely in your browser. It has no server of its own, so there is nowhere for your camera, your music or anything about you to be sent, and no account to make.

The camera

  • The camera is used only while a score is open and only to see the gesture that turns the page.
  • Video is processed on your device, by face-detection software that is downloaded to the device and runs there. No video frame, image or face measurement ever leaves it.
  • Nothing is recorded. Frames are examined and discarded as they arrive.
  • No face is identified or recognised. What is measured is the angle of your head and whether an eye is closed — not who you are.
  • The camera stops when you close the score or leave the page, and your browser decides whether to grant it at all.

Your scores

  • PDFs you open are stored in your browser, on your device, so that they are still there next time and still work with no network.
  • They are never uploaded. There is no copy of them anywhere else.
  • Alongside each score the app keeps the page you were last on, a small picture of its first page for the chooser, and the date the songs were grouped into a set.
  • You can remove any score, or a whole set, from the list inside the app. Clearing your browser's site data for this site removes all of it.

Google Drive, if you use it

Opening scores from Google Drive is optional. Nothing below happens unless you press that button and sign in.

  • The app asks Google for one permission, drive.file. That covers only the files you yourself select in Google's own file picker. The app cannot see, search or list the rest of your Drive, and never asks to.
  • The files you pick are downloaded from Google straight to your device and stored like any other score. They do not pass through any server belonging to this app, because there is none.
  • The sign-in token is held in memory for as long as the page is open and is never written to the device. Reloading the page discards it.
  • You can withdraw this at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Nothing obtained from Google Drive is used for advertising, sold, or given to anyone, and it is not used to train any model.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising, no third-party scripts following you.
  • No cookies are set by this app.
  • No account, no name, no email address, no contact details.
  • Your calibration and settings are saved in your browser on your device and are not readable by anyone else.

Where it is served from

The app is published as static files on GitHub Pages. Serving any web page involves the host seeing the request for it; GitHub's handling of that is covered by GitHub's privacy statement. Google sees the sign-in and the file downloads if you use Google Drive, under Google's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects nothing from anybody, of any age, so there is nothing held about a child either.

Changes

If this ever changes — in particular if the app gains a server, an account or any form of analytics — this page will say so and the date above will change before the change ships.
